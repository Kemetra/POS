Direction B — cart-first cashier workspace
A calm, precise clinical retail instrument. The cart is the work; money is the loudest thing on screen; state is always named in words, never only in colour. Everything else stays quiet.

  • Two zones only. Start side (right, RTL): scan bar + cart. End side (left): totals and the action ladder. No nav rail, no dashboard tiles.
  • Fixed chrome is capped at 48 px shell (44 at 1024). One optional status strip (38 px) only when the system is not normal.
  • Totals and the primary action never scroll. Only the cart body scrolls; header and footer of the cart stay pinned.
  • 1024 is re-laid, not scaled: pane 300, no index column, rows 48 with barcode shown only on the active or selected line, shorter labels.
  • Recovery and approval surfaces are docked or replace the work area; the transaction stays on screen and identifiable.

Visual hierarchy — what the eye finds, in order

  1. Money due — 44 px mono total beside the 56 px Pay action.
  2. The cart — largest surface; the last-scanned line is tinted and tagged.
  3. Attention — a status strip or banner in words + icon, only when something is not normal.
  4. Transaction identity — sale number, start time, line and piece counts in one quiet line above the cart.
  5. Next safe action — exactly one teal filled button per screen. Recovery screens may switch it to the safe step.
  6. System state — chip in the shell: online, offline, syncing, locked, uncertain.
Rule: one filled teal button per screen. If two things look primary, one of them is wrong.

Status treatment

متصلغير متصلمزامنة3 بانتظار المزامنةمقفلةبانتظار موافقة المديردفع غير مؤكدفشل الدفعالبيع مكتملالإيصال لم يُطبع
  • Three tiers: chip in the shell (always), strip under the shell (system-wide, non-blocking), banner / surface in the work area (this transaction).
  • Offline is ink, not red: it is a mode, not an error. Red is reserved for definitive failure.
  • Uncertain = amber + diagonal hatch + "?" icon. Failed = red + "×". They never share a look.
  • Silence means normal: no green banners for routine success; a toast at most.

Dialogs & recovery surfaces

Docked panel — approval, held sales. Slides over the end side; cart stays readable under a light scrim.
Work-area replacement — payment recovery. Transaction summary stays; the ledger is replaced by the recovery surface.
Full lock — only terminal identity and "a sale is preserved". No amounts or lines.
  • No centred password-style modals. Every surface names the action, the affected object and the safe exit (Esc).
  • Focus ownership, scanner routing and shortcuts follow the existing Retail Tower input contract — not defined here.
